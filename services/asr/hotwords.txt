# Format: one phrase per line, written naturally (no spaces needed). Blank lines and
# lines starting with "#" are ignored. core/hotwords.py normalizes each phrase into the
# per-character form sherpa-onnx expects, so this file stays readable and reviewable.

# What belongs here: short phrases a person actually says to the robot in 功能1 near-field
# dialogue, plus proper nouns the model has no reason to know. What does NOT belong here:
# ordinary conversational words -- every listed phrase raises the chance of a false hit on
# audio that never contained it, so the vocabulary is kept deliberately small and specific.
